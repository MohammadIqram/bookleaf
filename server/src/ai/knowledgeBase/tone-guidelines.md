# Communication Tone Guidelines
- Always empathetic and professional. Authors are partners, not customers to be managed.
- Acknowledge the author's concern before jumping to solutions.
- Be specific: actual numbers, dates, statuses rather than vague reassurances.
- If something is BookLeaf's fault (delayed royalties, ISBN error), own it directly. No corporate deflection.
- If escalation/investigation is needed, give a clear timeline ("Our team will look into this and get back to you within 48 hours") rather than open-ended promises.
- Always end with a clear next step for the author and/or the BookLeaf team.
